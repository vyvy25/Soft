What we cover

Five consulting services, clearly scoped

Technology assessment

A structured review of your current stack, architecture, processes, and team capabilities. Output: written findings and ranked recommendations.

Architecture review

We examine your system design for scalability, security, and maintainability risks. Identify problems before they become production incidents.

Stack selection

Framework-agnostic evaluation of technology options against your specific requirements, team skill set, and long-term roadmap.

Digital transformation planning

A phased roadmap for modernizing your technology operations — prioritized by business value, not technical preference.

Vendor evaluation

Structured comparison of vendor proposals — technical capabilities, integration effort, total cost of ownership, and lock-in risk.

Our approach

What you actually get from a consulting engagement

01

Discovery sessions

Structured interviews with your technical leads, product team, and relevant stakeholders. We understand the problem before proposing anything.

02

Documentation & analysis

We document the current state — architecture diagrams, dependency maps, identified risks — and present it back to you for alignment.

03

Recommendations

Written, prioritized recommendations with clear rationale. Options-based when relevant — you make the final call with full context.

04

Implementation handoff

Optionally, we can carry the recommendations forward into execution — through our development or QA services, or alongside your internal team.

Questions

Common questions about our consulting service

Every engagement includes discovery sessions, a documented analysis of the current state, and written recommendations with rationale. Depending on scope, this may also include architecture diagrams, a technology roadmap, or a vendor evaluation matrix. You own all deliverables. The format and depth are agreed upfront during scoping.
Both models work. We can conduct an independent review and present findings externally — useful when you want an unbiased perspective your internal team can't provide. Or we can work alongside your engineering and product teams as a technical partner, embedded in your process. We recommend the independent model when objectivity matters, and the embedded model when speed of implementation matters.
Typical deliverables include: a current-state assessment document, an architecture or process diagram, a prioritized recommendations report, and (if applicable) a vendor comparison matrix or migration roadmap. Every deliverable is written to be understood by a non-technical business stakeholder, not just engineers. We don't deliver slide decks that go unread.
A focused architecture review typically takes 2–3 weeks. A comprehensive technology assessment for a mid-size organization takes 4–6 weeks. Ongoing advisory retainers run month-to-month. We scope the engagement before it starts so you know what you're committing to — no open-ended billing.
