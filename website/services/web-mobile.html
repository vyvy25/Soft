How we work

A process built for accountability, not meetings

01

Discovery

We scope requirements, map technical constraints, and define success metrics before writing a line of code. Output: a written specification you approve.

02

Architecture

System design, data models, API contracts, and infrastructure plan. We document decisions so your team inherits something maintainable.

03

Design

UI/UX design aligned to your product goals. Prototype-first — you see and approve flows before development begins.

04

Development

Iterative sprints with weekly demos. You always know what's been built and what's next. No black-box delivery.

05

QA

Testing runs in parallel with development — not after. Functional, regression, performance, and security checks before every release.

06

Launch & Support

Deployment, handoff documentation, and ongoing support. We stay available as your product scales and requirements change.
