Construction

2×2 grid of 32px squares on an 80px artboard. 6px outer padding, 4px gutter, 8px corner radius.

Geometry

Artboard 80 × 80 Square 32 × 32 · radius 8 Outer padding 6 Gutter 4 (between squares) Stroke 3 (outlined squares)

Color logic

Diagonal pairing. Solid squares sit at opposite corners, outlined squares at the other diagonal. One accent only. The top-left tile carries #EE4E34 — Gamma's signature warm. Soft/Flex duality. Solid = Soft (filled, present). Line = Flex (open, adaptive).
(05)

Don't

Five things that break the mark. Keep these in mind across every application.

Don't Fill every square with accent. Reserve #EE4E34 for one tile only.
Don't Remove the corner radius. The 8px round is signature — sharp corners read as a different brand.
Don't Thin the stroke. Outlined squares always sit at 3px — anything thinner loses weight against the solid tiles.
Don't Swap the diagonal pairing. Solid tiles must stay top-left + bottom-right. The composition reads as off-balance otherwise.
Don't Rotate or skew the mark. The 2×2 orthogonal grid is the entire idea — tilting destroys the reading.
Do Use the mark exactly as drawn. Solid accent · outline · outline · solid white, on the official diagonal.
