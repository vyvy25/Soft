Rules of thumb

Quick guidelines to keep typography consistent across the product.

Base weightbody

Use 500 Medium for body text — it gives the interface presence without feeling heavy on dark backgrounds.

500not 400
Display trackingheadings

All sizes 28px and above get -0.015em letter-spacing. Body and below stay at 0em.

-0.015em≥ 28px
Italic accentdecorative

Use italic sparingly to highlight one or two words inside large headlines — never for body or emphasis in running text.

italic+ accent color
Eyebrowslabels

Eyebrow labels always go in UPPER with 0.1em tracking. They sit above headings, never alone.

UPPER+0.1em
Line heightvertical rhythm

Body uses 160% (Gamma default). Headlines drop to 102 – 110% for compact display.

body: 160%display: 102%
Color hierarchycontrast

Primary text in white, secondary text in muted #949a9a, never both at the same weight.

#FFF / #949A9A2-level only
