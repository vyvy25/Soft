(05)

Do & Don't

Rules to keep the card recognisable across the product.

Do

Keep titles to 1–2 lines

Aim for 3–6 words. Long titles break the visual rhythm of the 3-up grid and push the CTA below the fold.

Don't

Don't add a second action

One primary CTA only. Adding "Read More" + "Contact Sales" splits attention and weakens the card.

Do

Use photographic media

Real, contextual photography (people, workspaces, devices). Keep tonal range broad — avoid flat illustrations.

Don't

Don't mix card heights

Description blocks must have equal line counts within the same row. Truncate or rewrite to keep CTA aligned.

Do

Match radius hierarchy

Container radius (20px) is always larger than image radius (12px). This nested geometry is signature.

Don't

Don't fill the CTA by default

The accent fill is reserved for hover. A pre-filled CTA at rest overpowers the card composition.
