Gamma. Foundations

Visual primitives for the Gamma consulting interface — color, space, shape, elevation, motion. Typography is documented in a separate spec.

(01)

Color

Dark-first palette. A single warm accent does the heavy lifting; everything else stays quiet.

Background #1A1A1A · --color-bg
Surface #2E2F2F · --color-surface
Accent #EE4E34 · --color-accent
Muted #949A9A · --color-muted
White #FFFFFF · --color-white
Accent · 10% rule

The orange accent never exceeds ~10% of any composition. Reserved for CTAs, links, italic emphasis and active states.

Two-level contrast

Text uses only white (#FFF) or muted (#949A9A). Never tint to other grays — it breaks the editorial calm.

Surface for elevation

Cards and inputs use #2E2F2F instead of shadows. Dark-on-dark elevation by tone, not by depth.

(04)

Elevation

Two shadow tokens only. Most depth on dark UIs is communicated via surface tone, not blur.

Flat no shadow · surface only
Soft --shadow-soft
Accent glow --shadow-accent
(05)

Motion

Three durations, one out-ease for interactions and one in-out for slow reveals. Everything else stays still.

Fast
--dur-fast · 150ms · ease-out
Base
--dur-base · 280ms · ease-out
Slow
--dur-slow · 520ms · ease-in-out
