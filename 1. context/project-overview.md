# Project Overview — Market Pay Landing Page

## About the company
Market Pay is a Canadian based fintech founded in 2021 by a former executive with deep experience across financial services. The company specializes in cross border B2B payments, with a particular focus on Asia markets.

Their mission is to simplify international trade by giving businesses a secure, unified infrastructure to collect, hold, and pay funds globally.

## Services
1. **International Payment Gateway** — Accept payments via credit cards and local payment methods.
2. **Multi currency Virtual Accounts** — Open local virtual accounts to collect funds like a local, avoiding unnecessary FX conversion fees.
3. **Global Payouts** — Same day payouts to bank accounts and mobile wallets, with transparent foreign exchange rates.

## Key differentiator
Compared to competitors, Market Pay offers low and transparent FX and flat transaction fees, with a deep focus on Asia markets through a dedicated core system.

## What is this project
Build a marketing landing page that presents Market Pay's services officially online. This is the company's primary web presence at launch. No signup or login flow is required for now (may be added in the future). A contact page will let prospects leave inquiries.

The visual direction is clean and simple, similar in style to Tazapay.

## Target audience
The client described the audience as "business clients," which is too broad to design against. Based on Market Pay's services and Asia focus, the realistic primary audience is:

**Primary: Cross border SMBs and mid market businesses in or trading with Asia**
- Ecommerce sellers shipping into or out of Asian markets
- SaaS and digital product companies billing international customers
- Import and export businesses managing supplier and buyer payments across borders
- Marketplaces and platforms that need to pay sellers or contractors globally

**Decision makers visiting the site**
- **Founders and CEOs** at companies with 10 to 200 employees, looking for a payment partner
- **Finance leads** (CFO, Head of Finance, Finance Manager) evaluating FX costs and payout speed
- **Operations leads** dealing with payment friction day to day
- **Developers** doing technical due diligence on the payment gateway

**What they care about**
- Lower and clearer FX fees than incumbents
- Speed of payout (same day matters)
- Trust and licensing (is this fintech legitimate)
- Coverage (which countries and payment methods are supported)
- Ease of integration

**Implications for design and writing**
Tone should feel professional and trustworthy, not playful. Copy should lead with concrete numbers (fees, speed, coverage) over abstract benefits. Visuals should communicate scale and reliability without feeling corporate or stiff.

## Goal of this project
Present Market Pay's services officially online and convert qualified visitors into contact form inquiries. Success looks like a clear, credible first impression that earns a "let's talk" from finance and ops leaders at cross border businesses.

## Success focus: discoverability and first impression
The client did not set hard conversion targets, and at launch the priority is being found and being understood. The team should focus on:

**1. SEO discoverability**
- Page titles, meta descriptions, and OG images set for every page
- Clear H1 and heading hierarchy that matches how people search ("cross border payments Asia," "B2B payment gateway," "global payouts")
- Fast load times and mobile friendly (Google ranking factors)
- Schema markup for company info and services

**2. AI search visibility (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude)**
- Write copy in plain, factual sentences that AI engines can quote directly
- Include explicit statements of what Market Pay does, who it serves, and how it differs, so AI can summarize the company correctly
- Use a clear FAQ section with real questions and direct answers
- Avoid burying key facts inside images or animations

**3. First impression quality**
- Visitor understands what Market Pay does within 5 seconds of landing
- The page feels credible enough that a finance lead would forward it to a colleague
